# Variable-density mixing needs Rayleigh–Taylor or Richtmyer–Meshkov data

**Category:** data generation
**Priority:** unset — the team has not set a priority for the idea
**Status:** open, waiting on data

## Context

Idea: [Variable-density mixing](../docs/ideas/regime_specific/variable_density_mixing.md). Its row in `docs/ideas/README.md` links here, so the idea is not picked up for building until this issue is resolved; the change that then builds it deletes this file.

Mixing width, growth laws, the molecular mixing fraction, variable-density second moments and anisotropy need two fluids of different density and their volume fractions.

## Why it is not built

No dataset has two fluids, volume fractions or an accelerated interface.

## What would unblock it

A Rayleigh–Taylor or Richtmyer–Meshkov dataset with volume fractions.
